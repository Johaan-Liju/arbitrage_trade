# Hypothetical paired-trade rules

These deterministic research rules use settlement observations as assumed fills, not executable orders. No trades are sent. All configured costs are user assumptions, never official exchange fees.

1. Partition by dataset and both exact contract identities, expiry dates and immutable specification versions. Use the implemented left-minus-right normalized spread z-score: current observation excluded from the preceding lookback sample, sample standard deviation, and at least `min_observations` prior eligible pairs. Undefined/zero variance and failed quality gates cannot signal. Prices, normalization references and specifications must be available at each decision, not just at the final as-of cutoff.
2. With no position or pending order, inclusive `abs(z) >= entry_z` requests entry. Positive z shorts A and buys B; negative z buys A and shorts B. One paired position at a time, no pyramiding or same-observation reversal. Signals while occupied are explicitly skipped.
3. An order attempts the next dated observation, strictly after its decision on BOTH legs. The next missing paired date cancels entry explicitly. Other failed entry attempts are unfilled; exits remain pending and are reissued after failed attempts. No interpolation, forward fill, same-observation execution or end-of-series liquidation. Both legs fill together by assumption. Publication time is the hypothetical fill confirmation time; each leg's observation time is retained. A fill must also be known at the run cutoff.
4. Use the smallest equal gross-weight package from the least common multiple of whole-gram lot sizes, within `max_lots` per leg; otherwise skip. Quote notional = raw quote × lot grams / quote grams × whole lots. Do not use normalized quotes for cash P&L. Report signed gross grams, fine-gold grams and 999-equivalent exposure. Matching weight does not match purity, delivery, settlement, tax or funding terms. The active API and UI require identical exact expiries. Legacy allow_calendar configuration cannot bypass this rule; differing expiries are unsupported comparisons.
5. Exit decisions occur at inclusive `abs(z) <= exit_z`, or when calendar days since entry reach `max_holding_days`, or at the safe-exit buffer, in that priority order: expiry safety, holding limit, convergence. Holding limit requests an exit; delayed/missing fills may exceed it. The execution deadline is the earliest safe-exit, exact expiry, specification valid-to, or day before tender for either leg. Start requesting exit `max_age_days` before that deadline. Never enter on/after that trigger. The final as-of truncates data; it does not create a future-aware liquidation signal.
6. Each fill must pass quality, publication, specification validity and daily volume AND OI participation gates for each whole-lot quantity. Orders older than `max_age_days` are rejected/reissued. Daily volume/OI is only a liquidity proxy. No bid/ask depth, queueing, partial fills, legging risk, margin or default simulation is implemented.
7. On each leg at entry and exit, itemize brokerage (`fee_bps`, shared legacy setting), per-leg exchange/clearing, tax and other bps, plus a per-leg fixed INR charge. Each bps component applies to absolute raw-quote notional. Adverse slippage is a separate notional cost; quotes remain unchanged to avoid double counting. Defaults are hypothetical 5 bps brokerage, 10 bps slippage, and zero other costs. Zero means omitted, not tax exemption. Costs must be finite and nonnegative. Entry threshold must exceed exit threshold.
8. Gross P&L is the sum of signed raw-quote notional changes. Net realized P&L deducts both entry and exit cost items and slippage for CLOSED trades only. Open entry costs and open marks are separate. Unknown marks withhold total performance. Open positions are never included in realized trade counts or realized net P&L. Equity includes realized P&L plus open raw mark less incurred entry costs, without estimated exit costs. Drawdown is absolute INR from the reference-capital peak, not a margin return.

The ledger retains original signal and decision timestamps, per-leg observed/available fill times, exact identities/spec versions, quantities, raw quotes, cost items, residual exposure and exit reasons. Rejections identify unfilled orders, missing observations, warm-up failures and occupied-position signals. Delivery and settlement differences remain unresolved even with identical expiry dates. Results are hypothetical research, never guaranteed arbitrage.
